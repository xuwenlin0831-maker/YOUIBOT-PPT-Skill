## Selection rules

- Prefer slides 1–14 when building a formal report with chapters.
- Use slides 15–29 to vary content density while preserving the company system.
- Use slides 35–46 when the content contains evidence that must remain editable.
- Copy individual icons or robot assets from slides 47–50 into another duplicated template slide. Do not deliver asset-library slides unchanged.
- Avoid three consecutive slides with the same composition. Alternate general content, evidence, image, diagram, and section slides where the narrative supports it.
